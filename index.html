About Arihant World Villas

Arihant World Villas is an ambitious riverfront township in Chowk, Karjat, offering meticulously designed G+1 storey villas with maximum space, servant quarters, and wide balconies. Residents enjoy access to an elite Gymkhana and ultra-premium amenities, including a private forest, Olympic-sized swimming pool, sports arena, resort, and an integrated shopping mall.

The 63-acre township boasts excellent connectivity via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway and the upcoming Chowk railway station. Top schools, hospitals, and landmarks like ND Studios and Morbe Dam are just minutes away. Live an exquisite lifestyle surrounded by the serene landscapes of Karjat.
